## Why Hire a Business Consultant?

A business consultant brings:
1. **Objective perspective** — They see your business from the outside
2. **Experience** — They have worked with dozens of businesses and know the patterns
3. **Accountability** — They ensure your plan gets executed

1. **Proven track record** — Check their Google reviews and client testimonials
2. **Clear methodology** — They should have a structured approach
3. **Industry expertise** — They should understand your specific market
4. **Communication skills** — They should be able to explain complex concepts simply
5. **Results orientation** — They should focus on measurable outcomes
