## What is Business Consulting?

Business consulting is a process where a professional consultant guides business owners in building strategy, marketing, branding, and business development. In Israel, small and medium businesses make up over 90% of the economy.

## Model M — A Unique 5-Step Method

1. **Mikud (Focus)** — Identifying the business goal and strategic direction
2. **Mitzuv (Positioning)** — Defining competitive advantage and branding
3. **Mapa (Roadmap)** — Building a detailed action plan with timelines
4. **Mentoring** — Close guidance and support throughout execution
5. **Minof (Leverage)** — Realization and business growth
